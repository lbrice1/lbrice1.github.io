This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

## Data collected

The site itself collects no personal data. GitHub, which hosts the site, records technical information about each request, such as the IP address, in its server logs. GitHub handles these data under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Local storage

The light and dark theme switch stores your choice in your browser's local storage. The value stays on your device and is never sent to a server. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Third parties

Fonts and scripts are served from this site. Pages make no requests to other servers until you follow a link, for example to a publisher, Google Scholar, or GitHub.
